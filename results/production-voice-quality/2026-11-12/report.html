How the composite is calculated

Within each metric: average repetitions within eligible scenarios, average eligible scenarios within each number, then average eligible numbers equally within the provider. Composite is the weighted sum of twelve normalized metric scores. Latency is separate and is the median of individual qualifying events. N/A is excluded from applicable denominators.

Graded scores are converted from 0–5 to 0–100. Binary checks use 100 for a pass and 0 for a failure. Stop-time quality is 100 at 300 ms or less, 0 at 3,000 ms or more, and linear between those anchors.

CategoryWeightMetrics and weights
Audio quality20%Voice tone and clarity 15%, Speech integrity 5%
Turn-taking and reliability40%Caller interruption avoidance 10%, Interruption recovery 10%, Interruption stop time 10%, Infrastructure reliability 10%
Response quality25%Response relevance 10%, Spoken organization 10%, Unnecessary repetition 5%
Conversational adaptation15%Conversational style fit 5%, Emotional acknowledgment 5%, Conversational cue handling 5%

Only the production AI interaction is assessed. Navigation, recorded menus, human handoffs and testing-agent idle check-ins are excluded. N/A means the call did not contain the evidence required for that metric; it does not count as a pass or failure. Coverage beside each chart shows scored calls / selected calls.
