English speech recognition: public and private results

Accuracy depends on the recordings. Available-results comparisons show usable public clips and selected private recordings. The fixed-set ranking uses identical clips for eligible models. Each reference word has equal weight; dataset scores remain visible separately. Word error rate (WER) counts incorrect, missing, and extra words per reference word. Lower is better.

Provisional accuracy ranking. The eight private speaker recordings come from four conversations and contribute 37.6% of the reference words. This dataset mix is not yet validated as representative of production traffic. Final accuracy alone does not determine the best provider for a voice agent; compare deadline accuracy, timing, and reliability.

Scoring and comparison fixes applied
  • Standalone punctuation left by normalization, including ellipses, is no longer counted as a word. Raw transcripts remain unchanged.
  • Combined rank pools errors and reference words across a frozen public and private comparison set. Hiding models does not change scores or ranks.
  • Public and private scores contribute to combined rank. FLEURS remains separate. Latency comparisons are separated by finalization method; later extra text still counts.

These corrections use the original saved evidence. They do not depend on new provider experiments.

How to read this benchmark

Combined ranking, equal weight per word. Add substitutions, insertions, and deletions over the frozen shared public clips and eight private recordings, then divide by their total reference words. This is not an average of dataset percentages. FLEURS is excluded. Punctuation-only tokens are removed on both sides after normalization. All-available scores remain visible. Successful recoveries can improve final accuracy.

Coverage is part of the result. The English suite contains 1,000 Pipecat clips, 180 FLEURS clips, and 8 private recordings. Nova-3 has 144 usable FLEURS clips out of 180; Gradium and Reson8 have 15 each. Other models have no saved FLEURS measurement. Missing clips add neither errors nor words. A low score on less data is not proof of a better model.

What receives a rank. Completed runs with all Pipecat and private items attempted and usable results on every frozen ranking item receive a combined rank. Missing results never shrink the comparison set; incomplete models stay visible without a combined rank. Failed or excluded items stay visible in coverage. AssemblyAI receives a rank when its full run and evidence checks are complete. Trial Pipecat duplicates and private excerpts are not added on top of full runs.

Model selection. Flux uses English. Nova-2, Flux Multilingual, Whisper, GPT-4o Mini, and Chirp 2 are omitted. GPT-4o Transcribe is the single OpenAI entry. Nova-3 and Flux remain distinct model families; the two Speechmatics operating modes remain visible.

p50, p90, p95, p99. Respectively, 50%, 90%, 95%, and 99% of observations fall at or below these values, using linear interpolation. Percentiles are calculated from individual observations, never averages of dataset or worker percentiles. Sparse samples make tail percentiles less reliable.

Interim timing. The saved metric is the first nonempty partial update after speech ends. It is not time to the first transcript from speech start. “Unavailable” means no eligible observation; it does not mean the model cannot stream interim text. Public timing uses only Pipecat first attempts that passed the original validity checks. FLEURS does not contribute to these timing columns.

Final and word timing. Last final text received after speech end measures the last final-text event relative to speech end. Later extra text can extend it; negative values mean final text arrived earlier. A finalization signal is a request, not proof that final text is already available. Private finalized-word delay measures receipt of correctly aligned final words relative to delivery of their word-end audio. Private word timing retains the original saved word mapping and denominator; its observations are not recomputed by this WER correction. These are different measurements and are never pooled together. Accuracy-only exclusions do not remove original timing observations. Stream completion additionally waits for the provider's completion signal.

Inworld: corrected full rerun. The selected run sends endTurn and closeStream at the speech boundary, without streaming artificial silence afterward. All 1,000 public clips and eight private recordings completed successfully. Every final transcript, including any repetition, remains in accuracy scoring. The older trailing-text diagnostic is historical evidence, not the source of the current scores.

Reference provenance. Pipecat’s current documentation describes Gemini-generated, human-reviewed reference transcripts (source). The review coverage for our exact frozen dataset revision has not been verified here. A model can benefit from matching the reference generator’s wording; this is a possible bias, not a demonstrated cause of its score. Private reference generation has not been independently verified here. Pooling datasets does not remove reference bias. Independent listening review, with model names hidden, remains follow-up work.

Limits and evidence. Network time is included. Runs used different dates and concurrency settings. Gradium and Gemini use session handoffs for long recordings; AssemblyAI uses 60 ms packets. References and word boundaries were not independently checked by listening. This page uses saved scores, not live progress. No confidence intervals or overall accuracy-plus-speed score are invented.
