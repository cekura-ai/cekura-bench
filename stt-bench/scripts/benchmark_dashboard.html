About this benchmark

Word error rate (WER). Incorrect, missing, and extra words divided by total reference words. Each clip uses its first valid completed result, including a retry if needed. We sum word counts rather than averaging clip percentages. Usable coverage differs between models and is shown in the table.

Finalize latency. Time from sending the final speech frame to the provider’s acknowledgment. The charts use the median (p50); p90 means 90% of measured samples were at or below that time. Timing uses usable results and includes network travel.

Different acknowledgment signals. Nova responds to Finalize; Flux signals EndOfTurn; OpenAI completes the committed transcription item; Scribe returns the committed transcript; Gemini completes generation after activity end; Cartesia signals flush_done. These signals are not a shared time-to-final-word metric.

Reliability and cost. All models processed the same 1,000 frozen clips. Some results were excluded by existing scoring rules. Failed attempts and retried clips are different counts. Cost is the saved full-run estimate, including retries where accounted for; smoke tests, setup, and Vercel compute are excluded. Missing prices are unavailable.

Limits. Source transcripts and automatic speech boundaries have not been independently reviewed by listening. Reviewed entity accuracy and statistical confidence intervals are unavailable. Observed differences do not establish a statistically significant winner. This is our own method, not a reproduction of Coval or Pipecat scores.

Saved evidence. A fixed snapshot of 10 completed runs, without a rolling time window or live status. Both Speechmatics models are excluded. The CSV includes deadline observations, completion timings, reliability, and source IDs for further analysis.

About the private benchmark

Accuracy. WER counts substituted, inserted, and missing words divided by reference words. Accuracy can use a successful retry. Coverage is shown per model. Recovered accuracy includes later successful attempts; Nova-2 retains its original first-attempt word timing.

Finalized-word delay. Time from the reference word ending to receipt of its correctly aligned finalized transcription, including the network route. Only valid first attempts contribute. This is different from the public benchmark’s finalize acknowledgment latency. p50 is the median; p95 means 95% of measured words arrived within that delay.

Timed-word coverage. The share of all reference words with a valid first-attempt delay. Incorrect, missing, ambiguous, and invalid-timing words are excluded. Read delay alongside coverage.

Failures. Completed recoveries replace earlier missing accuracy results after archive verification. Historical failed benchmark and smoke attempts remain counted. Models still undergoing recovery retain their previous results until verified; see the status above.

Limits. Supplied reference transcripts and word times were not independently reviewed. These are observations on eight recordings, not a general model ranking. Gemini resets context at nine-minute handoffs. Completion signals differ between providers; completion delays in the CSV should not be ranked across providers.

Costs and evidence. Unknown prices remain unavailable. CSV cost estimates separate benchmark and smoke audio and exclude compute and earlier recovery runs. Recovery status reflects the latest saved local evidence. The HTML contains aggregate results, without private transcripts or audio.

About the short tests

Coverage and failures. Smallest, Sarvam and Inworld each completed 20/20 clips: 10 private excerpts and 10 Pipecat clips. Soniox stopped on its first public request because the provider reported an exhausted balance; no private clips were attempted. Gradium and Reson8 each completed the same 31 public clips: 16 Pipecat, 8 general FLEURS and 7 FLEURS clips with annotated details.

Timing. Deadline WER measures text available at 0, 250, 500 and 1,000 ms after speech ends. Final-text delay and completion are different measures. Gradium’s finality depends on its end-of-stream signal and includes the silence tail; other providers use different finalization signals. Do not rank these delays as equivalent text-arrival times.

Limits. These sample sets differ from the full Pipecat and private long-recording benchmarks. References were not independently reviewed by listening. Costs and confidence intervals are unavailable. The page embeds aggregate results only.
