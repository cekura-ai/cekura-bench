What the numbers mean

  • WER: incorrect, missing, and extra words divided by reference words, pooled across valid transcripts. Incorrect transcripts remain included.
  • TTFT: first nonempty text received minus the start of the first audio packet. Connection setup is excluded. The first text may be partial or final.
  • TTFS: the last change to the completed final transcript minus delivery of the speech-end frame. A finalization acknowledgment or later socket close does not replace that text timestamp.
  • Early final text: negative TTFS is displayed as zero and remains signed in the saved results.
  • Google Chirp: controlled finalization is unsupported here. Its observed final-text delay is separate from controlled TTFS.
  • Unavailable: missing text, invalid pacing, incomplete streams, and unsupported protocols are not zero latency.

Limits and recovery

Automatic preparation, not an independent listening review. Speech ends were estimated with Silero VAD. The supplied transcripts were retained; the 206 clips are automatically prepared speaker segments. These are four conversations, not 206 independent calls. Confidence intervals resample conversations.

The overall ranking uses verified full runs on its original fixed dataset and word weights. Turn clips are not added to that score. Its whole-recording and public timings use earlier measurement contracts. This follows Coval’s timing approach, but does not claim identical hosts, data, transports, or results.
